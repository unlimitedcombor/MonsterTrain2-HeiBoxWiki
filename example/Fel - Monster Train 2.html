Flavor Text

The brilliant and resourceful commander of the Banished, Fel vows to free Heaven from her brother's tyranny. Whether or not her wounded heart will ever heal, however, is yet to be seen.

Artist

Genevieve Bega

Fel is the default Champion of the Banished clan. All three of her paths revolve around  Valor, which can grant her enormous damage and defense if used properly. She also starts with  Multistrike 1 regardless of path, which lets her double the additional from each point of  Valor. Her starter card is Just Cause.

Strategy

[edit]

Each of Fel's paths requires a different strategy, but they can still be mixed effectively. Vanguardian wants to stay at the front of the floor, and can massively buff all units on the floor. Unchained requires moving her frequently and can allow for extremely high damage. Savior provides greater survivability, and can protect other units if fully upgraded.

Vanguardian

[edit]

Vanguardian essentially turns Fel into a variant of Firebrand, granting  Valor not only to herself but to the entire floor. This can be immensely powerful if she is placed in front of units which can take advantage of this extra damage, such as those with  Multistrike or  Sweep. Each additional level of Vanguardian doubles the  Valor applied, increasing the damage output further.

The main weakness of this path is the risk of being killed before you can stack up enough  Valor to survive. Unlike Firebrand, Fel cannot be upgraded with  Titanite, and even at Vanguardian III she has only 40. Mixing with either of the two paths can help with this. Unchained allows you to start with lots of  Valor already, and Savior lets you gain additional  Armor when you need it.

Unchained

[edit]

When using Unchained Fel, your goal is simple: move her as much as possible. Just Cause is essentially required for this, and so this path is rarely a good idea if using the Pyre of Dominion. In the early game, simply put her on the top floor next to a Shield Steward, or a banner unit if you happened to start with a useful one, and swap their position over and over. Later on, your goal should be to find units with  Shift triggers such as Battle Dancer or Demonic Fledgling, put them next to Fel, and constantly switch their positions.

Just Cause may only be a starter card, but it's actually one of the best cards you can have for this path. Adding  Twinstone,  Emberstone, or similar upgrades that will let you play it more often can be a good idea. It can even be worth making more copies with the Mysterious Mirror event or Lifemother's Pyre, and putting them in the spell box from Wondrous Boxes can lead to an absurd number of  Shift triggers. Rising Rage is also useful, especially since it lets you overstack a floor with  Shift units. Hall of Mirrors can be strong as it allows for a second copy of Fel with her stored permanent  Valor, but the copy does not provide permanent  Valor to Fel's card in your deck, despite the tooltip indicating permanent  Valor has been gained on both copies of Fel. With a good build, it's possible to start the fight against Seraph with hundreds or even thousands of  Valor.

Unchained II grants a negligible benefit, adding only a few points of and . It is only worth taking in order to get to Unchained III, which adds an additional stack of  Multistrike. This technically only applies once you have  Valor 75, but by this point in the game you should be starting every battle with far more than that, so this is generally irrelevant. If mixing paths, it's best to start with one level of Unchained and then take two levels of another path, rather than pathing into Unchained later or stopping at Unchained II.

The main weakness of the Unchained path is that Fel is very vulnerable until she's had a chance for her  Valor to grant  Armor. After that point, she's essentially invincible, but it may be worth going Unchained I/Savior II to make sure that she doesn't die early. That said, if you have any way to protect her, such as putting her on the middle floor and leaving another unit on the top floor as bait for flying bosses, it's usually worth it to go for the extra  Multistrike. Starting the battle against Seraph with a unit that deals 1000x3 almost guarantees a win.

This path goes well with all the Hymnists, which provide even greater benefits to her absurdly high damage. It is also one of the biggest beneficiaries in the game of the Karmic Censer artifact, as it doubles her scaling. Note, however, that this does not apply to Celestial Cascade, which will boost her  Valor in battle but not permanently.

Savior

[edit]

Savior allows Fel to immediately move to the front of the floor, gaining  Valor and  Armor in the process. It is especially effective when used with powerful but vulnerable units like Demonic Fledgling and Death's Dancer, as Savior III applies  Armor to the entire floor. This is especially effective against Seraph Aeternus-The Dominant, which would otherwise kill these units quickly.

This path mixes well with either of the other two. Unchained increases the  Armor gained from each use of the Savior ability, and benefits from the additional  Shift trigger. Vanguardian is best used when Fel is at the front of the floor, and the  Armor from Savior helps her avoid being killed before she can stack up enough  Valor to survive. However, going for pure Savior allows Fel to apply  Armor to the entire floor, which is often worth giving up the benefits of the other two paths.

Unlike normal  Valor, which only "refills"  Armor up to its maximum value, Savior simply applies  Armor equal to  Valor regardless of the current amount of  Armor. If you wait for Fel to gain  Armor from her  Valor normally before using Savior, then she will have  Armor equal to twice her  Valor, which can make her extremely hard to kill.
