The Savage Spearman is an enemy in Monster Train 2. It is a basic backline enemy in Rings 2-3.

Strategy

[edit]

The Savage Spearman has low , but its damage is higher than most other early-game enemies. Having a way to kill them before they attack is very useful, making cheap spells with low damage like Torch, Flash Freeze and Glimmer effective in the early game. The same spells are effective against many other early-game enemies as well.

If you cannot kill them directly, units with  Sweep or spells with Advance can also finish them off quickly. Jackstrips and Rules of Containment will eliminate them as soon as they change floors.
