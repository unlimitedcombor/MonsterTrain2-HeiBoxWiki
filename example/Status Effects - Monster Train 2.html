From Monster Train 2

The following is a list of Status Effects in Monster Train 2 that can be applied to units. For effects applying to cards, see Card Effects.

Effects

[edit]

Effects are generic effects represented by yellow icons.

Effect Description
 Armor Damage is dealt to Armor before health. Each point of Armor blocks one point of damage and is then removed.
 Buffet This unit can be  Eaten multiple times.
 Burst Attacks an additional time per stack. Remove 1 stack after each attack made this way.
 Cardless This unit was not summoned from a unit card. It won't go to the Consume pile and can't gain  Endless.
 Dualism Doubles the stacks of  Armor and Buff and Debuff status effects applied by this unit.
 Endless When this unit dies, return its card to the top of the draw pile.
 Fate Link Can not be killed until all units with Fate Link are at 1.
 Fragile If this unit loses health, it dies.
 Graft Unit has equipment grafted to their body. When they die, the graft is severed for the rest of combat and the equipment goes into your hand.
 Haste Moves directly from the first floor to the third floor.
 Immobile Can't move between floors or change position on this floor.
 Inert This unit cannot attack unless it has  Fuel.
 Live to Fight Instead of entering the Pyre room this unit moves back to the bottom floor.
 Mageblade +1 per stack for each Magic Power on the floor.
 Mass +2 per stack. Powers Fleshweaver's abilities.
 Multistrike Attacks an additional time per stack.
 Pyre Bound Battles the Pyre Heart once a turn.
 Quick Attacks before enemy units during combat.
 Relentless Combat in this room continues until all enemies are defeated. This unit cannot be rooted.
 Shard Powers Solgard the Martyr's abilities.
 Shell At the end of turn, remove from the floor to remove Shell from the front unit with Shell. When all Shell is removed, the unit will Hatch.
 Silence Disables Triggers and Abilities.
 Sniper Prioritizes attacking the back enemy unit.
 Stasis Cannot attack. Cannot be damaged or targeted in any way.
 Steelguard Cannot lose more than 15  Armor per hit.
 Suppressed This unit does not attack on its turn.
 Sweep Attacks all enemy units.
 Titanite Incoming damage reduced by half.
 Titanskin 1 damage reduction per stack.
 Trample When attacking, excess damage is applied to the subsequent enemy unit.

Triggers

[edit]

Triggers describe conditions which activate particular effects. They are represented by circular purple icons above a unit's and .

Trigger Description
 Ability On-demand trigger for specific unit actions. Has a Cooldown between uses.
 Action Triggers at the start of this unit's turn.
 Armored Triggers when  Armor is added to this unit.
 Artificer Triggers when Equipment is attached to any ally of this unit.
 Ascension Triggers when moving up a floor.
 Celebrate Triggers after defeating a boss.
 Conjure Triggers when this unit's  Ability is activated.
 Eaten After the next round of combat, this unit will be eaten by the front unit without an Eaten ability. Triggers when this happens.
 Enchant Other units on the same floor gain a bonus.
 End of Turn Triggers at end of turn.
 Equipped Triggers when Equipment is attached to this unit.
 Etch Triggers when a card is Consumed on this floor.
 Extinguish Triggers on death.
 Full Moon Active only during the Full Moon.
 Gorge Triggers when this unit eats a unit.
 Harvest Triggers when a unit on this floor dies.
 Hatch Triggers once all of the egg's Shell is removed. Summon a new unit and apply the egg's upgrades, equipment and stat changes to the summoned unit.
 Hellborne Harvest Triggers when a friendly unit on this floor dies.
 Hunger Triggers when a unit with  Eaten is summoned.
 Incant Triggers when you play a spell on this floor.
 Inspire Triggers when you gain on this floor, once per .
 Loop Triggers when this unit returns to the bottom floor.
 Mooncycle Triggers when the moon changes phases.
 New Moon Active only during the New Moon.
 On Team Turn Triggers when this unit's team starts fighting.
 Rally Triggers when a friendly unit is summoned on this floor.
 Reanimated Triggers when a unit is revived from  Reanimate.
 Rejuvenate Triggers when healed, even at full health.
 Resolve Triggers after combat.
 Revenge Triggers when being hit.
 Shift Triggers whenever this unit moves.
 Siphon Triggers when an enemy unit on this floor dies.
 Slay Triggers after dealing a killing blow.
 Strike Triggers when attacking.
 Summon Triggers when played.
 Vanguard Applies when the unit is at the front of the floor.

Buffs

[edit]

Buff effects are generally positive for their target and are represented by green icons.

Buff Description
 Avarice When a unit with Avarice deals unblocked damage, gain equal to stacks of Avarice.
 Burnout Counts down every turn. When Burnout runs out, the unit dies.
 Conduit +1 Magic Power on this floor per stack.
 Damage Shield Nullify the next source of damage.
 Fuel Negates the effects of  Inert. Loses 1 stack every turn.
 Infested +1 per Infested stacks on the Train at turn start.
 Lifesteal When a unit with Lifesteal attacks, it restores health equal to the damage dealt.
 Rage +2 per stack. Decreases every turn.
 Reanimate When a unit with Reanimate is killed, it will be revived with 1 and lose 1 stack of Reanimate.
 Regen Restores 1 health per stack at the end of turn. Decreases every turn.
 Soul Used by certain abilities.
 Spell Shield Nullify the next damage from a spell or ability.
 Spikes When a unit with Spikes is attacked, the attacker takes 1 damage per stack.
 Stealth Not a target in combat. Decreases each turn.
 Troop Troop gains bonus and per stack. Additional Troop stacks on a floor are added to any existing Troop. When this unit loses health equal to the granted by one stack, it loses a Troop stack.
 Valor +1 per stack. Raise  Armor to stack count if this is the front unit after combat.

Debuffs

[edit]

Debuff effects are generally negative for their target and are represented by red icons.

Debuff Description
 Corruption Unit takes 1 damage per stack at the end of turn. Stacks remain across turns.
 Dazed As long as a unit is Dazed, it can't attack or use any  Action or  Resolve abilities.
 Decay Unit takes 3 damage per stack at the end of turn. Decreases every turn.
 Emberdrain At the start of turn, you lose 1 for every stack of Emberdrain. Decreases every turn.
 Frostbite Unit takes 1 damage per stack at the end of turn. Decreases every turn.
 Heartless A unit with Heartless cannot be healed.
 Malaise -1 and -1 per stack. cannot be reduced below 1.
 Melee Weakness The next time the unit is attacked, it takes that much damage again for each stack of Melee Weakness.
 Mute Disables Triggers and Abilities. Decreases every turn.
 Pyregel +1 incoming damage per stack.
 Reap Unit takes 1 damage per stack of after combat ends. Does not decrease.
 Rooted Prevents the next time a unit would move between floors. Bosses are immune.
 Sap -2 per stack. Decreases every turn.
 Spell Weakness The next time the unit is hit by a damage spell, it takes that much damage again for each stack of Spell Weakness.
 Unstable If a unit's is less than or equal stacks of Unstable at the end of combat, they die. On death, this unit explodes, dealing damage equal to stacks to enemies on the floor.
 Witherbloom This unit and adjacent units take 1 damage per stack at the end of turn.
